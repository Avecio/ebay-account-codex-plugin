# Privacy Policy — Peacock Scout eBay Connector

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Peacock Scout eBay Connector is a personal-use application used by its owner to connect an eBay account to local software and approved AI-assisted workflows through eBay's official APIs.

## Information accessed

The application may access eBay account information only after the account owner grants permission through eBay OAuth. Depending on the permissions granted, this can include inventory and offers, account settings, order and fulfilment information, and basic account identity information.

## How information is used

Information is used only to provide the requested eBay account-management functions, such as viewing inventory, reviewing orders and fulfilment status, and assisting with seller workflows.

The application does not sell eBay account data or use eBay account data to train machine-learning models.

## Storage and security

eBay OAuth credentials and tokens are stored outside the public source-code repository. Local deployments store credentials on the account owner's own computer. Access to the MCP interface is protected with an application secret.

When the owner chooses to connect the application to an AI service or other approved client, information requested through the connector may be transmitted to that service as necessary to fulfil the owner's request and will be subject to that service's applicable privacy terms.

## Sharing

The application does not intentionally disclose eBay account information to third parties except where necessary to provide a service explicitly chosen by the account owner or where required by law.

## Revoking access

The account owner can revoke the application's eBay authorization through eBay or remove locally stored OAuth tokens.

## Contact

For privacy questions about this personal-use application, contact the application owner through the GitHub repository that hosts this policy.
